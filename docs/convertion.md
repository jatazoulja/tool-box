# Unit conversion reference

## Length

- **Metric:** millimeter (mm), centimeter (cm), meter (m), kilometer (km)
- **Imperial/US:** inch (in), foot (ft), yard (yd), mile (mi)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 in = 2.54 cm
  - 1 ft = 0.3048 m
  - 1 yd = 0.9144 m
  - 1 mi = 1.60934 km

---

## Mass / Weight

- **Metric:** milligram (mg), gram (g), kilogram (kg), metric ton (t)
- **Imperial/US:** ounce (oz), pound (lb), short ton (US ton)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 oz = 28.3495 g
  - 1 lb = 0.453592 kg
  - 1 US ton = 907.185 kg

---

## Time

- **Units:** millisecond (ms), second (s), minute (min), hour (h), day, week, month, year
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 min = 60 s
  - 1 h = 60 min = 3600 s
  - 1 day = 24 h

---

## Temperature

- **Units:** Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Kelvin (K)
- **Common conversions:**
  - °F → °C: \((°F - 32) \times \frac{5}{9}\)
  - °C → °F: \((°C \times \frac{9}{5}) + 32\)
  - °C → K: \(°C + 273.15\)

---

## Area

- **Metric:** square meter (m²), square kilometer (km²), hectare (ha)
- **Imperial/US:** square foot (ft²), square yard (yd²), acre
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 ft² = 0.092903 m²
  - 1 yd² = 0.836127 m²
  - 1 acre = 4046.86 m²
  - 1 ha = 10,000 m²

---

## Volume

- **Metric:** milliliter (mL), liter (L), cubic meter (m³)
- **Imperial/US:** teaspoon, tablespoon, fluid ounce (fl oz), cup, pint (pt), quart (qt), gallon (gal), cubic inch (in³), cubic foot (ft³)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 L = 1000 mL
  - 1 US gal = 3.78541 L
  - 1 ft³ = 28.3168 L

---

## Speed / Velocity

- **Units:** meters per second (m/s), kilometers per hour (km/h), miles per hour (mph), knots
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h
  - 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h
  - 1 knot ≈ 1.852 km/h

---

## Pressure

- **Units:** pascal (Pa), kilopascal (kPa), bar, atmosphere (atm), pound per square inch (psi), torr (mmHg)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 bar = 100,000 Pa
  - 1 atm = 101,325 Pa
  - 1 psi ≈ 6894.76 Pa

---

## Energy / Work

- **Units:** joule (J), kilojoule (kJ), calorie (cal), kilocalorie (kcal), watt-hour (Wh), kilowatt-hour (kWh), British thermal unit (BTU), electronvolt (eV)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 cal = 4.184 J
  - 1 kcal = 4184 J
  - 1 Wh = 3600 J
  - 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J

---

## Power

- **Units:** watt (W), kilowatt (kW), horsepower (hp)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 kW = 1000 W
  - 1 hp ≈ 745.7 W

---

## Force

- **Units:** newton (N), kilonewton (kN), pound-force (lbf), dyne
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 lbf ≈ 4.44822 N

---

## Density

- **Units:** kilogram per cubic meter (kg/m³), gram per cubic centimeter (g/cm³), pound per cubic foot (lb/ft³)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³

---

## Frequency

- **Units:** hertz (Hz), kilohertz (kHz), megahertz (MHz), gigahertz (GHz)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 kHz = 1000 Hz
  - 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz
  - 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz

---

## Angle

- **Units:** degree (°), radian (rad), gradian (gon)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 360° = 2π rad
  - 1 rad ≈ 57.2958°

---

## Data / Storage

- **Units:** bit (b), byte (B), kilobyte (KB), megabyte (MB), gigabyte (GB), terabyte (TB)
- **Common conversions (binary):**
  - 1 B = 8 b
  - 1 KB = 1024 B
  - 1 MB = 1024 KB
  - 1 GB = 1024 MB
  - 1 TB = 1024 GB

---

## Electric current

- **Units:** ampere (A), milliampere (mA)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 A = 1000 mA

---

## Voltage

- **Units:** volt (V), millivolt (mV), kilovolt (kV)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 V = 1000 mV
  - 1 kV = 1000 V

---

## Resistance

- **Units:** ohm (Ω), kiloohm (kΩ), megaohm (MΩ)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 kΩ = 1000 Ω
  - 1 MΩ = 10⁶ Ω

---

## Capacitance

- **Units:** farad (F), microfarad (µF), nanofarad (nF), picofarad (pF)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 µF = 10⁻⁶ F
  - 1 nF = 10⁻⁹ F
  - 1 pF = 10⁻¹² F

---

## Inductance

- **Units:** henry (H), millihenry (mH), microhenry (µH)
- **Common conversions:**
  - 1 mH = 10⁻³ H
  - 1 µH = 10⁻⁶ H

---

## Luminous intensity

- **Units:** candela (cd)
- **Illuminance:** lux (lx), lumen (lm) (context-dependent conversions)

---

## Currency (example)

- **Units:** USD, EUR, PHP, JPY, etc.
- **Conversion:** depends on current exchange rates (dynamic, not fixed)
